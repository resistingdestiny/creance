challenger-ai-cuts-monthly.json
  Monthly count of announced United States job cuts where the employer itself
  cited artificial intelligence as the reason, as compiled by Challenger, Gray
  and Christmas. Forty months, May 2023 to August 2026, 188,000 cumulative.
  Compiled into a workbook by a research pass, then reduced to this series.
  The category did not exist before May 2023, so 2022 is absent rather than zero.
  Several monthly cells are derived from published year to date figures rather
  than from a published monthly figure. The series carries no per month flag
  saying which, so no month here can be told apart on that basis.

Known limits, all of which belong next to any chart drawn from it:
  Self reported by employers and contested by economists.
  Announcements, not separations. Announced is not the same as happened.
  National and industry level. It is NOT occupation coded, so it cannot be
  bound to a BLS occupation group without an assumption that would need its
  own defence.
  Zero of 162 or more New York WARN filings ticked the artificial intelligence
  box introduced in March 2025.
  Verizon, Block and TCS each explicitly denied that headline cuts were AI driven.

Measured against our own index over the tracked window: correlation with the
smoothed excess margin is -0.44 for computer and mathematical, about zero for
construction and farming. Differenced month on month it falls to -0.21 and
nothing at three or six month leads, so the level relationship is substantially
a shared trend. It is context, not a predictor.
